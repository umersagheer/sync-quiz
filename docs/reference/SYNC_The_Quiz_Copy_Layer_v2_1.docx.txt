SYNC  ·  THE QUIZ  ·  COPY LAYER v2.1
SYNC HEALTH
The Quiz — Copy Layer
v2.1 · customer-facing copy + education layer + reveal PDP


What this is
The customer-facing copy layer for the SYNC quiz. Sits on top of the Backend Mapping v2.1 engine spec — every trigger, every rule (R1–R8), every substitution unchanged. This document changes only what the customer sees and how the reveal is laid out.
What changed against FINAL v2.1
Voice: hybrid register applied deliberately. Direct on questions, options, and reveal decision copy — that's where the customer is doing work or making a choice, and directness converts. Warm on the opener, interstitials, the read, and the recommend-less reveal — those are emotional beats where warmth earns its place. The "that's not X, it's Y" construction is retired throughout — warmth without the formula.
Education: five education screens added, one per branch, sitting between the discriminator and the cross-signal questions. Plain-language biology, no product mention, no sell. Signposted with grey callout boxes.
Recognition lines: added after specific branch answers where a real clinical pattern fires. Red-bar callouts. Fire on the same screen as the answer, not as their own screen.
Reveal (Screen 10): rebuilt as a PDP-style page. Base card with 4 benefit chips + 3-line paragraph + price. Supporting protocol card same shape when adjunct fires. Plan selector defaulted to 3 months with clinical framing line locked. Sticky footer with running total + primary CTA visible throughout scroll.
What did not change
The engine. Every trigger, every rule, every routing decision, every result assembly — all inherited from Backend Mapping v2.1. Zarar builds engine logic against that doc. This doc governs the words and the reveal layout.
How to read
Customer copy is larger and indented. Grey italic notes are build instructions. Red-bar callouts are recognition lines (same-screen). Grey box callouts are education screens (own screen after branch).

5.1  ·  The read  ·  warm  ·  opens the page
Here's what we see, [name].
One of 10 read variants fires based on primary lane × cross-signal. Sharp pairings evaluated first per Backend §5; if none fire, the lane fallback fires. Every customer gets exactly one read. Warm register — this is the emotional beat before commerce.
Sharp pairings  ·  fire when the cross-signal is there
Body composition + under 7 hrs sleep
You want your body to change — and you're sleeping under seven hours. Most of your growth hormone releases in deep sleep, which means short sleep quietly works against the change you're putting effort into. It's the same pattern in most people who plateau despite doing the work.
Visceral fat + high stress
Firm, deep fat around your middle — and the last few months running on empty. Those track together more often than most people are told. Sustained stress drives your body to store fat there specifically. It's the fat pattern that responds to your nervous system, not your fork.
Systemic recovery + under 7 hrs sleep
You don't bounce back the way you used to, and you're under-sleeping. Both of those run on the same overnight window. If sleep is short, recovery can't complete — and if recovery can't complete, the next day starts already behind.
Flat energy + high stress
Flat energy and months of running on empty. Your body burns through its cellular energy reserves faster under sustained stress than under almost anything else. The tiredness has a biology behind it, not a scheduling problem.
Sex drive + high stress
Your sex drive has shifted — and the last few months have been heavy. Those two are more connected than most people are ever told. Desire is one of the first things to drop when the system is under sustained load.
Lane fallbacks  ·  fire when no sharp pairing does
Recovery

Metabolic

Skin & longevity

APPENDIX  ·  VOICE PRINCIPLES
The hybrid rule
Direct register for: questions, options, engine-facing copy, plan selector, sticky footer, and reveal decision copy. These are where the customer is either doing work (answering) or making a choice (buying). Directness converts.
Warm register for: Screen 1 opener, welcome interstitial, the read (Screen 10 top), and the recommend-less reveal. These are the emotional beats where the customer is being addressed, not asked to do anything.
Warm-adjacent for: Screen 8B stress question, Screen 8C 90-day capture, day-90 line. Sit between the two — warmth in the framing, direct in the ask.
Retired throughout
The "that's not X, it's Y" construction. Warm without the formula — no reframing, no "you're not imagining this," no "it's not your fault." Warmth means addressing the customer as a person. It doesn't mean telling them how to feel about their own experience.
What Amelia and Reid sign
Reid signs the associative framing on Screen 10 (the read pairings) and the RESTORE lower-confidence positioning. Both already signed in Backend v2.1. Amelia signs the base card and supporting protocol card paragraphs when written, and the pairs-well-with one-liners. Voice register is Patrick's call.
